Support for Dimensions Planner, the pixel-dimension and aspect-ratio planner for iPhone and iPad.

What it does

Plan source and canvas dimensions in pixels, compare fit with centered fill/crop, and keep named presets by project. It calculates sizes; it does not resize image files or modify display/game settings.

Quick example

A 4032 × 3024 source in a 1920 × 1080 canvas fits at 1440 × 1080, leaving 240 canvas pixels on each side. Fill & crop keeps 75% of the source area and crops 378 original source pixels from the top and bottom.

Editing dimensions

Enter ungrouped values from 0.001 to 10000000. Locale decimal marks, dot decimals and Arabic decimal digits/marks are supported. Grouping separators are rejected. A target ratio lock updates the paired dimension. Finish an edit with Done; valid edits also save when leaving the app. Incomplete or invalid text does not replace the previous completed draft.

Your projects

Save to project keeps source, target and placement. Search/filter presets, rename them, reorder through Edit or Move earlier/later, and confirm deletion. Undo restores the latest deletion during the session. Deleting a preset does not clear the active canvas.

Backups

Settings provides JSON export/import through Files. Import adds unknown preset IDs and preserves existing local versions and the current canvas. Files must be valid schema-1 workspaces no larger than 2 MB. Export before removing the app. Copies in Files or device backups are separate; keep them in a trusted location.

If saving fails

Keep the app installed and check available device storage. An unreadable or future-schema workspace is preserved and protected against writes; Settings can export the original file. Do not send a complete backup unless you choose to and it is needed for the agreed support process.

Report an issue

Include the app version/build, device and OS version, the steps taken, and what you expected. Screenshots and measurements are optional. The app does not attach plans or diagnostics automatically.
