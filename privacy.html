Privacy

Effective 6 November 2026.

Provider and contact

Dimensions Planner is a syymza app. Privacy/support contact: daniele@duck.com.

Information kept on your device

Source dimensions, target canvas dimensions, placement choices, saved preset names, project names and display preferences are stored locally. There is no in-app account, advertising, analytics SDK or developer-operated backend. Plans are not automatically transmitted to the developer, and the app does not track you across apps or websites.

Sharing and exports

Share specs provides the current dimensions, scale and fit/crop measurements to a destination you choose through Apple's share sheet. JSON exports contain saved presets and the last completed canvas. Exported files do not have encryption added by this app. The Files provider you select may sync them under its own settings and terms. The app does not automatically send an export to the developer.

Device backups

Apple device backups may include app data according to your device and iCloud settings. App-managed automatic cloud sync is not implemented. Device backups and provider-held exports are managed separately from local app storage.

Retention, deletion and Undo

Deleting a preset removes its saved record. The latest deletion is temporarily retained in memory for Undo during the current app session; it is not a persistent recovery archive. Deleting a preset does not clear the current canvas. Removing the app removes its local storage. Earlier exports and device backups can contain previously deleted presets. Importing an old backup can restore them. Manage exported copies through Files, the chosen provider or Apple's backup settings.

Support correspondence

If you choose to email support, your email address and the information you include are handled by the email providers involved and used to respond to your request. The app's mail link supplies a product-name subject, without automatically attaching plans, backups or diagnostics. Include only information you choose to provide. Contact the support address for questions about support correspondence or deletion requests concerning it.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical request information, including visitor IP addresses, to operate and secure the service. See GitHub’s privacy statement. These pages use no analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms or cookies set by this site.

Changes

This policy will be reviewed if app features, services or data flows change.
